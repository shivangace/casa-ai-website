RBI Master Direction · IT Governance · April 2024
RBI AI Governance

Sections 50–58 of the RBI Master Direction on IT Governance, Risk, Controls and Assurance Practices (April 2024). Mandatory for every bank, NBFC, payment institution, and regulated financial entity in India that deploys AI agents in any customer-facing or decision-making capacity.

No foreign competitor — Lakera, Arthur AI, Guardrails AI, Protect AI — has built these controls. CASA-AI built them from the actual Master Direction text, not a summary. Every control has a corresponding test.

Why this is the moat: Every Indian bank that deploys an AI agent for credit scoring, KYC, fraud detection, or customer service is legally required to comply with these controls. CASA-AI is the only infrastructure layer that enforces them at runtime.

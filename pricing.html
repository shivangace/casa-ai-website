Frequently asked

Questions we always get

How does the license key work without internet?+
The license key is an Ed25519-signed payload. The engine validates it using a public key compiled into the binary at build time — zero outbound calls. Proven with iptables DROP ALL outbound: engine starts, validates license, processes evaluations correctly in a fully network-blocked container.
What happens when I hit the agent limit?+
The session registry enforces the limit at registration time. Agent number N+1 receives HTTP 429 immediately. No UI, no bypass possible. The limit is extracted from the signed license payload — it cannot be modified without invalidating the signature.
Does my data reach CASA-AI servers?+
No. The engine runs entirely inside your VPC. No prompts, reasoning chains, tool call data, or audit logs reach CASA-AI infrastructure. License validation uses the embedded public key — zero outbound network calls. This is verifiable by running iptables DROP ALL outbound and confirming the engine operates normally.
Can I upgrade mid-month?+
Yes. Email shivangace001@gmail.com and we'll issue a new license key for your upgraded tier within minutes. The old key continues to work until the new one is deployed. There's no downtime — swap the CASA_LICENSE_KEY environment variable and restart the container.
What does "2 months free" mean on annual plans?+
You pay for 10 months and receive 12 months of access via the license expiry date in your signed key. BASIC annual: ₹40,000 total (₹3,333/mo effective). ENTERPRISE annual: ₹5,00,000 total (₹41,667/mo effective). BIG INFRA annual: ₹12,00,000 total (₹1,00,000/mo effective).
Which payment methods are accepted?+
All Indian payment methods via Razorpay: UPI, NEFT, RTGS, netbanking, Visa/Mastercard, RuPay, EMI. GST invoice provided for all purchases. For CUSTOM tier enterprise contracts, bank transfer and purchase orders are accepted.
